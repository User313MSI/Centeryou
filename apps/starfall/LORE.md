# Starfall Academy — Series Bible

## Premise
Above the clouds floats **Solenna**, a city built on the backs of sleeping giants of light. Its sky is woven from stars, and when a star falls, a piece of the world dims. At the **Starfall Academy**, young Keepers are trained to catch falling stars and stitch them back into the night.

## Main Characters
- **Lumi** (14) — a girl born from a comet. Hair like drifting stardust, glowing freckles. Determined, clumsy on the ground, graceful in the sky. She has never stitched a star without tearing the sky.
- **Kaito** (15) — a fox-eared mechanic who builds star-catchers from junk. Deadpan humor, secretly afraid of heights. Lumi's best friend.
- **Master Orin** — an old Keeper with a beard of constellations. Speaks in weather proverbs.
- **Nix** — a small star-sprite, mute, mischievous, made of light. Communicates in chimes.

## Season arc (planned)
1. **"The Falling Hour"** — Lumi's first solo catch goes wrong; a whole constellation, the Silver Heron, falls over Solenna.
2. **"The Sky Remembers"** — Lumi and Kaito climb the Starbridge to find the Heron's heart, and Lumi learns why the sky tears under her hands.

## Tone
Warm, wonder-driven, Ghibli-ish adventure beats. Comedy from Kaito's gadgets and Nix's antics.

## Format
- Animated programmatically (Canvas 2D, 60 fps, vector characters, particle starfields).
- Original procedural soundtrack (WebAudio) with per-scene moods and SFX.
- Each episode ~5 minutes, subtitle dialogue (silent-film style with rich music).
