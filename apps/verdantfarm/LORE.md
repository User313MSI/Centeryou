# Verdant Farm — World Bible

## The Story
Generations ago, the Verdant Valley was the breadbasket of the kingdom — until the Great Wither, a slow drought curse, emptied it. You are **Ivy Bloom**, the last descendant of the Bloom family, the original keepers of the valley. You return to the ruined family farm with one seed pouch, a stubborn heart, and your grandmother's almanac. Every crop you grow feeds the soil's memory. Every animal you raise brings back a voice to the valley. The Great Wither is not broken by war — it is broken by harvest.

## Characters
- **Ivy Bloom** (you) — young farmer-heir, optimistic, covered in mud.
- **Granny Alba Bloom** — appears in letters and memory vignettes; her almanac holds secret crop knowledge.
- **Rook** — a talking scarecrow animated by valley magic; gives quests and terrible puns.
- **Pim** — a hedgehog merchant who trades rare seeds for Moonpetals.
- **Old Marlow** — the ferryman; the valley bridge rebuilds as you level up.

## Animals (unlock & produce)
- **Chicken Henrietta** → Eggs (fast, low value)
- **Cow Clementine** → Milk (slow, steady)
- **Sheep Nimbus** → Wool
- **Goat Beppo** → Goat Cheese
- **Bee Queen Aster** → Honey (passive, boosts adjacent crops)
- **Alpaca Duchess** → Fine Fiber (late game luxury)

## Crops (with lore tiers)
- **Wheat** — the starter grain that never abandoned the valley.
- **Carrot** — rabbit-shaped moonlight collectors, Granny called them.
- **Tomato** — red lanterns that ripen at dusk.
- **Corn** — tall singers; when the wind passes, the valley hums.
- **Pumpkin** — festival fruit; the Wither's first undoing.
- **Strawberry** — sweethearts of the eastern plot.
- **Moonpetal** — a magical flower; currency for rare trades with Pim.

## Progression Systems
- **Plots**: unlock and upgrade (regular → rich → golden soil).
- **Barn & Silo**: capacities that upgrade.
- **Quests via Rook**: chains that reveal the lore and reward Moonpetals.
- **Collection (Almanac)**: every crop/animal/product ever harvested is catalogued with a lore line.
- **Day/Night cycle**: visuals shift; some crops grow faster in daylight, Moonpetals only at night.
- **Weather**: rain waters crops (growth boost), rare "Blessing of the Valley" event doubles yield for 30s.

## Tone
Cozy, warm, hopeful. Pastoral fantasy. No timers that punish; the game respects the player's pace.
